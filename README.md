# ✈️ Telugu Top Trumps

**A card game celebrating the literary legends of Telugu literature!**

## 📖 About the Game

Telugu Top Trumps is an interactive web-based card game featuring over 200 renowned Telugu writers and poets. Players compete by comparing statistics of literary figures across different categories like books written, awards won, poems composed, and cultural impact.

🎮 **Play live**: [Play](https://tubular-starship-336c1e.netlify.app)

[🚀 Play Telugu Authors Game](https://tubular-starship-336c1e.netlify.app)

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Choose Game Mode**: Single player vs AI or multiplayer with friends
2. **Select Category**: Pick your strongest attribute from your current card
3. **Compare Values**: Higher value wins both cards
4. **Handle Ties**: Tied cards go to a pot - winner of next round takes all!
5. **Win Condition**: Collect all cards to become the Telugu literary champion

## 🎯 Game Features

- **216+ Telugu Authors**: From classical poets like Nannayya and Potana to modern writers like C. Narayana Reddy
- **Single Player Mode**: Challenge the AI with strategic category selection  
- **Multiplayer Mode**: Play with friends in real-time rooms
- **Tie Mechanics**: Build up card pots during ties for high-stakes rounds
- **UTF-8 Support**: Beautiful rendering of Telugu script and names
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices

## 🏆 Categories

Compare literary legends across these metrics:
- **📚 Books**: Total published works
- **🏅 Awards**: Major literary honors (Jnanpith, Sahitya Akademi, etc.)
- **📝 Poems**: Number of poems/compositions  
- **⏰ Span**: Years of active literary career
- **🌍 Translations**: Works translated into other languages
- **💫 Impact**: Cultural and literary influence (0-100 scale)
